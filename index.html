About this calculator

A quick tool for the three everyday percentage questions. Enter two numbers and all three results are calculated at once.

What each row answers

Percentage of a number
What is ○ percent of ●? For example, 20% of 150 is 30.
What percent
What percentage of ● is ○? For example, 30 is 20% of 150.
Change
By what percentage does ○ change to reach ●? From 150 to 180 is 20%, from 180 to 150 is about -16.67%. A negative value is a decrease.

How to use it

  1. Enter your two numbers in the ○ and ● fields. Decimals and negative values are fine.
  2. Read the results below. Each row repeats your numbers and highlights the computed value in green.
  3. Use the swap button to exchange the two numbers when the roles are the other way round.
  4. Add up to three calculators to compare different pairs side by side.

Results are shown in full, without rounding. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
